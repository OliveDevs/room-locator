Add an item guide

Fill in the details from the physical item and the checkout system. This form prepares an updated site file for review; it does not change the published site or the Room Locator console.

After downloading

  1. Review the new entry and upload the downloaded data.json to the site repository. Upload any local photo file at the path you entered.
  2. Update the Room Locator console inventory separately. The website cannot move equipment or add it to the console.
  3. Open the new guide and room view on the published site to confirm both match the physical item.
