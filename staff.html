For lending staff

Staff tools

Make a code on your phone and show it to the Room Locator's scanner. The console shows what will change, lights the old and new spots, and asks for the staff PIN before it saves anything.

If the shelf you pick already has things on it

Bring the console's changes into this website. On the console: Home, Down past the last picture, Select, then "Send to website" near the end of the map. Scan every page it shows.

What changes on the website

    The website changes only after someone uploads these two files to the site repository.

    Show this to the console

    1. Hold your phone about 10 cm from the console's scanner, screen bright.
    2. Type the staff PIN on the console.
    3. Check the screen and the lights, then press Select.

    Good to know

    • The PIN is checked on the console, never on this page. It starts as 1234. Change it on the console: press Down past the last picture on Home, Select, then go to the last spot on the map, "Change staff PIN".
    • Shelf numbers count grooves from the bottom. If a board was taken out for something tall, the console puts things on the board below.
    • This page reads the website's copy of the room. Moves made at the console since the last update are not shown here, so the console's own screen is the one to trust.
    • A new item added with a code exists on the console right away. Its guide page appears on this site after someone uploads the downloaded data.json, or after "From the console" brings it over.
    • A mistake on the console can be taken back there: Recent changes, then Undo the last change. Adding a new item cannot be undone on the console.
