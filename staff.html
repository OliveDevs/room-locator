              Manuals

              Everything the Room Locator does, for staff and for students. Version 2

              Print the manuals

              • Staff manual, ready to print 10 sheets of paper
              • Guide, ready to print 4 sheets of paper

              Printer settings, on any printer:

              • Letter paper
              • Two-sided, flip on long edge (most printers do this by default)
              • Actual size or 100%, not "Fit to page"
              • Booklet mode off: the file is already in booklet order
              • Colour or black and white, either works

              On a CMU campus printer (printing.andrew.cmu.edu) these are already the defaults, so upload the file and print. If the backs come out upside down on another printer, print again with flip on short edge.

              Then keep the sheets in the order they come out, fold the stack in half with the cover outside, and staple on the three small marks along the fold.

              Check everything

              Checks the website, the site key, the passcode and the console link.

                How to put in a new site key
                1. On a phone, scan the console's Staff page code (the last spots on its map, after the staff PIN). That way the console's link is renewed in the same step. A laptop works too, but then press Link the console afterwards.
                2. Sign in to GitHub with the account that owns the room-locator repository and open New fine-grained token ↗
                3. Repository access: Only select repositories, room-locator. Permissions: Contents, Read and write. Pick the longest expiration GitHub allows and note the date.
                4. Generate it, copy it, come back here and press the button below. Paste it with its date and the staff passcode.
                5. Press Check everything again. Every line should say OK.

                Student staff access

                Managers choose which tools student staff can use on the staff page. Untick one to hide it from them. Managers always see everything.

                Passcode and sign-out

                Staff passcode

                For student staff who need to save from a laptop. Phones don't need it: they sign in by scanning the console's Staff page code. It's separate from the console PIN. At least 10 characters: a few words together is easy to remember and hard to guess. Saving a new one signs out every device that signed in with the old one, so change it when a student leaves the job or loses a phone.

                Manager passcode

                For Lending's managers. It signs a device in as a manager, from the same box as the staff passcode, and keeps working when the staff passcode changes. At least 10 characters.

                Owner passcode

                Signs in from the same box as the staff passcode and keeps working when staff change theirs. At least 8 characters.

                Console link

                Lets a phone that scans the console's Staff page code save without typing anything. Redo it if the console or its card is replaced, or to sign out every phone that signed in by scanning the console (for example after a student leaves the job). Phones then scan the console again.

                Unlock the console with its PIN, then show it this code. You only see it once.

                Help
                • The console PIN starts as 1234. To change it on the console: press Down past the last picture on Home, press Select, then choose Change staff PIN.
                • Shelves are numbered from the bottom.
                • This page shows the website's copy of the room. If it disagrees with the console, the console is right, except for changes still waiting for the console (see Change history).
                • Saved something by mistake? If the console hasn't had it yet, press Undo next to it in the list under the code. It comes off the website too.
                • Made a mistake? On the console, open Recent changes, pick the change and press Select twice. To take an item out of Lending, open it under Edit an item.
                • Start over on the console clears every change and every item added there. To reset the website to match, use the locked section at the very bottom of Help.
